Our Core Training Programs

1. Hindi Playback & Contemporary Vocal Coaching

Focuses on developing a soft, warm, and resonant vocal texture for modern Hindi melodies and Sufi tracks. Covers breath control, microphone dynamics, emotional phrasing, and effortless transitions between chest voice and head voice.

2. Hindustani Classical & Tanpura Pitch Mastery

Build rock-solid pitch accuracy (Sur) with daily Tanpura scale matching, foundational Alankars, morning Riyaz routines, and classical ear training tailored for modern singers.

3. Acoustic Guitar & Vocal Accompaniment

Learn open and barre chords, fingerstyle patterns, rhythm strumming, and how to synchronize your singing effortlessly while playing the acoustic guitar.

Core Vocal Exercises & Studio Concepts

NG Vocal Resonance Exercise
A targeted humming technique using the "NG" sound (as in "sing") to place vibrations in the nasal/mask area, eliminating throat strain and unlocking a rich, warm tone.

Tanpura Scale Matching
Practicing sustained notes (Sa - Pa - Sa) against a continuous drone to calibrate ear-to-voice pitch accuracy and find your natural home scale.

Breath Support & Hiss Control
Controlled diaphragmatic breathing exercises (such as the sustained "S-hiss" and Yawn-Sigh) to sing long phrases smoothly without running out of air.
