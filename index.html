Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior singing experience?

No. Beginners start with basic pitch matching, posture, and gentle breath exercises.

What do I need for daily practice?

A Tanpura drone app or instrument and 20 to 30 minutes of quiet daily practice.

Are online and batch sessions available?

Yes, morning riyaz, evening student batches, and weekend sessions can be selected in the form.
