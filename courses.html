Structured Curriculum

Courses & Syllabi

Explore our acoustic music programs below. Click View Syllabus Notes on any course card to read the term-by-term learning breakdown.

Beginner – Advanced

Hindustani Classical Vocals

Develop natural resonance, pitch precision, and classical phrasing accompanied by live tanpura.

Duration: 6 Months  |  Sessions: 2 / Week

View Syllabus Notes

Modules 1–2: Breath alignment, omkar practice, sargam, and 12 foundational alankars.

Modules 3–4: Raga Yaman, Raga Bhupali, chota khayal bandish, and rhythmic taal alignment in Teentaal.

Beginner – Intermediate

Tabla & Rhythm Foundations

Learn authentic finger placement, tonal clarity on dayan and bayan, and rhythmic composition.

Duration: 6 Months  |  Sessions: 2 / Week

View Syllabus Notes

Modules 1–2: Sitting posture, basic strokes (Na, Tin, Ghe, Tirakita), and hand balance drills.

Modules 3–4: Teentaal, Dadra, Keherwa, traditional kayda variations, and simple tihais.

All Levels

Classical Sitar

Master expressive string bending, crisp mizrab strokes, and the melodic architecture of North Indian ragas.

Duration: 9 Months  |  Sessions: 2 / Week

View Syllabus Notes

Modules 1–2: Instrument tuning, Da-Ra bol patterns, left-hand fretting, and basic scales.

Modules 3–4: Alap, jod, Razakhani gat in Raga Kafi and Raga Khamaj, plus gentle meend exercises.

Beginner – Intermediate

Acoustic Guitar

Build clean chord transitions, fingerstyle independence, and dynamic strumming for solo and song accompaniment.

Duration: 6 Months  |  Sessions: 2 / Week

View Syllabus Notes

Modules 1–2: Fretboard orientation, open chords, rhythm notation, and alternate picking.

Modules 3–4: Barre chords, major/minor pentatonic scales, fingerpicking patterns, and acoustic arrangements.

All Levels

Piano & Music Theory

Combine expressive keyboard touch with staff reading, harmony, and structured ear training.

Duration: 6 Months  |  Sessions: 2 / Week

View Syllabus Notes

Modules 1–2: Hand independence, treble and bass clef reading, major scales, and triad inversions.

Modules 3–4: Pedal technique, chord progressions, classical etudes, and contemporary accompaniment.

Beginner – Intermediate

Bansuri (Bamboo Flute)

Cultivate warm air tone, subtle microtonal glides, and meditative phrasing on concert bamboo flutes.

Duration: 6 Months  |  Sessions: 2 / Week

View Syllabus Notes

Modules 1–2: Embouchure control, long-tone breath stability, full-note fingering, and sargam drills.

Modules 3–4: Half-note control (komal swaras), Raga Bhupali, Raga Desh, and lyrical folk melodies.
