Your 4-Step Vocal & Musical Progression

  1. Stage 1 (Month 1–2): Scale identification, Tanpura matching, posture alignment, and diaphragmatic breathing.
  2. Stage 2 (Month 3–4): Humming & NG resonance drills for soft, warm vocal tone and smooth note transitions.
  3. Stage 3 (Month 5–6): Complete song breakdown, feel & dynamics, and high-note control without vocal strain.
  4. Stage 4 (Month 6+): Condenser mic recording techniques and unplugged studio showcase performance.
