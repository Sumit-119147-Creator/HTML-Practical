Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own instrument for a trial class?

No. Our classrooms provide acoustic guitars, tabla pairs, upright pianos, tanpuras, and sitars for trial sessions. We also guide you on choosing a quality student instrument once you enroll.

What is the minimum age to join SwarSetu?

Students aged 6 and above are welcome across vocals, tabla, guitar, and piano. Sitar and bansuri batches typically start from age 9 due to instrument dimensions.

Are weekend batches available for college students and working professionals?

Yes. Dedicated Saturday–Sunday morning and afternoon batches run for all six courses.
